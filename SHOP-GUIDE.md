# Operational notes
- Sign in to /admin using one of the three configured team email addresses.
- Enter the number of unreserved packs available now. Saving 50 makes 50 packs available; existing reservations are separate.
- Pickup: Z-FRUITS shop, campus. No fixed hours; availability follows stock.
- Keep the Accept bookings option enabled during sales. Zero stock automatically prevents new bookings.
- Mark Collected after pickup; Cancel returns reserved packs to the available count.
- Students pay UGX 2,000 per pack at pickup. No online payment or automatic SMS is included.
- Refresh the dashboard for new bookings. Customer availability refreshes every 15 seconds and is rechecked atomically on booking.
- Keep the initial owner-private publication until ready to enable public access in Sites.
